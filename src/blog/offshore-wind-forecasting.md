Offshore wind is one of the fastest-growing renewable energy sectors. Turbines sit in environments where wind resource and wave conditions jointly drive safety, logistics, and production. Accurate meteorological and oceanographic forecasting is therefore critical for installation campaigns, vessel access, and ongoing operations and maintenance.

Monsun supports offshore wind clients with metocean intelligence that couples atmospheric and ocean models.

## Why offshore wind needs specialized forecasting

A strong wind day for generation can still be a no-go day for a crew transfer vessel if waves exceed limits. Conversely, calm seas with insufficient wind may idle technicians who cannot complete blade inspections. Operators need joint wind–wave guidance, not separate products that never meet.

Key forecast needs include:

- Hourly wind resource assessment at hub height
- Wave-induced load and workability estimates
- Short-range windows for jack-up and heavy-lift operations
- Seasonal outlooks for campaign planning

## Monsun’s role for offshore wind

We couple numerical weather prediction with ocean wave models to provide:

- **Hourly wind resource assessment** for planning and awareness
- **Wave-induced load estimation** relevant to access and structural context
- **Forecast-driven operation planning** tied to client thresholds

This integration improves efficiency and reduces maintenance downtime for offshore wind farms by aligning logistics with realistic metocean windows.

## Best practices for wind farm metocean programs

1. Calibrate models against local measurements early in the project life cycle.
2. Define vessel- and task-specific limits with marine assurance teams.
3. Deliver forecasts in the formats crews already use (briefings, APIs, dashboards).
4. Review forecast skill after major campaigns and update bias corrections.

These practices turn forecasting into a managed metocean consultancy capability rather than a static subscription.

## Looking ahead

As floating wind and deeper-water sites expand, metocean complexity grows. Currents, low-frequency motions, and remote logistics all raise the value of high-quality marine forecasting. Monsun continues to invest in predictive analytics and monitoring that keep pace with that growth.
