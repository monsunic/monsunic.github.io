Metocean consultancy is the practice of applying oceanographic and meteorological science to real-world offshore and maritime decisions. Clients hire metocean consultants when they need more than a generic weather report: they need site-specific guidance on waves, winds, currents, water levels, and how those conditions affect vessels, platforms, cables, and coastal works.

At Monsun, metocean consultancy sits at the center of our work. We translate numerical models, observations, and operational constraints into clear recommendations that operations teams can act on.

## Why operators need metocean consultancy

Offshore projects fail schedules when weather windows are misunderstood. A construction barge may sit idle for days if significant wave height thresholds are set too conservatively — or face damage if thresholds are ignored. Metocean consultancy closes that gap by combining:

- Historical climate statistics for design and planning
- Forecast guidance for day-to-day operations
- Risk framing that matches each client's vessels and procedures

Whether you are installing an offshore wind turbine, planning a subsea survey, or managing port approaches, metocean intelligence reduces uncertainty.

## Core services in a metocean consultancy engagement

A typical Monsun engagement may include:

1. **Site characterization** — long-term wave, wind, and current statistics for engineering design.
2. **Operational forecasting** — short-range marine forecasts tailored to workability criteria.
3. **Sea state monitoring** — near real-time awareness during critical operations.
4. **Decision support** — briefings that connect model output to go / no-go decisions.

These services are what people mean when they search for metocean consulting or metocean services: applied ocean expertise, not just raw data dumps.
